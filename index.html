? HOW TO PLAY: HOW DOES IT WORK

CLIP (Contrastive Language–Image Pre-training) was trained by OpenAI on about 400 million images paired with their captions from the internet. It learned to place images and text in the same "meaning space", so a photo of a dog ends up close to the words "a photo of a dog".

When you press START, the image and every description are turned into lists of numbers called embeddings. The app measures how similar the image is to each description, then turns those similarity scores into percentages that add up to 100%.

In BIG BRAIN mode the 1,437 descriptions were turned into embeddings ahead of time and saved with the app, so only your image needs processing. That's why it can compare against so many things in about a second!

BONUS CHALLENGES: What happens if none of the descriptions fit? What if two descriptions are almost the same? Can you find an image that fools the AI? Remember: the score only compares the options you give it, so a high score means "best of these", not "definitely correct".
